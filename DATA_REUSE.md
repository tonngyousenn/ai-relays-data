# Data Reuse and Attribution

This repository does not yet declare a blanket license for its contents or for third-party material.

Source records must state known attribution and reuse information, with a supporting URL where available. Unknown conditions are recorded as unknown rather than treated as unrestricted permission.

Link to the source and summarize the necessary facts. Do not copy whole articles, private datasets, or large bodies of third-party text into listings. Maintain original evidence URLs and observation dates.

A future repository license should specify which original contributions it covers. It must not imply that independently owned source material has been relicensed.
